## The file

`results.csv` holds one row for every company the edition names, in the order the edition page lists them: first the companies the Star Chart places, cell by cell, those scored on all five factors first, then every company Under the Radar, most named first. An empty value means the edition prints none for that company.

- `company`: The company's name, as the edition page prints it.
- `star_chart_cell`: The Star Chart cell the edition places the company in; empty for a company Under the Radar the Star Chart does not place.
- `group`: "leaderboard" for a company on the Leaderboard: named in enough answers to be scored on all five factors, with a CRISP score. "under_the_radar" for a company the engines named too rarely to score, listed with the answers that named it and no CRISP score.
- `crisp_score`: The CRISP score out of 100, printed only for a company scored on all five factors; empty otherwise.
- `consensus`: Consensus, out of 100, where the edition prints it.
- `reputation`: Reputation, out of 100.
- `influence`: Influence, out of 100, printed only for a company scored on all five factors.
- `source`: Source, out of 100, printed only for a company scored on all five factors.
- `presence`: Presence, out of 100.
- `named_in_answers`: How many of the edition's AI answers named the company.

These are results only. The data describes what AI engines said in the edition's answers; it does not explain why, and a low reading passes no judgement on a company.

## About the AI Authority Index

Buyers now ask AI assistants which company to choose before they search, and the answer often settles the shortlist. The Index shows, market by market, which companies those answers put forward, which they caution against, and which they leave out.

Each edition asks a market's own buyer questions of five AI engines, reads every answer, and places each company the engines name. Companies named in 30 or more answers form the Leaderboard, each scored on all five CRISP factors and placed on the Star Chart. Every other company named is listed Under the Radar, with the number of answers that named it.

No company can pay to be included or to be placed, and Third Hemisphere's clients are measured on the same instrument as everyone else. An edition passes the publication gate before it publishes, and it stands until the next edition of that market replaces it.

AI Authority Index, defined: https://thirdhemisphere.agency/glossary/ai-authority-index

## About CRISP

CRISP takes its name from the initials of its five factors: Consensus, Reputation, Influence, Source, and Presence.

CRISP breaks one question, how do AI engines treat this company, into five that can each be measured and acted on. Consensus asks whether the engines agree. Reputation asks whether they speak in the company's favour. Influence asks whether trusted third parties back what is said. Source asks whether the company's own site can be read and trusted. Presence asks whether the engines bring the company up at all.

How CRISP is collected and scored: https://thirdhemisphere.agency/crisp-methodology
