# AI Authority Index: Personal Lending, Edition 1 (results)

The published results of Third Hemisphere's AI Authority Index for personal lending, Edition 1, published on 19 October 2026. Each row is a company the edition places on its Star Chart, with the cell it sits in and the readings the edition page prints for it, read from 805 AI answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, and Perplexity. The full edition, with what each cell means, is at https://thirdhemisphere.agency/ai-authority-index/personal-lending.

## The file

`results.csv` holds one row per company, in the order the edition page lists them: cell by cell, companies scored on all five factors first, then every other company the Star Chart places. An empty value means the edition prints none for that company.

- `company`: The company's name, as the edition page prints it.
- `star_chart_cell`: The Star Chart cell the edition places the company in.
- `in_index`: "yes" for a company on the Leaderboard, scored on all five factors, which the page marks Leaderboard; "no" for a company placed on the market's answers alone, with no CRISP score.
- `crisp_score`: The CRISP score out of 100, printed only for a company scored on all five factors; empty otherwise.
- `consensus`: Consensus, out of 100, where the edition prints it.
- `reputation`: Reputation, out of 100.
- `influence`: Influence, out of 100, printed only for a company scored on all five factors.
- `source`: Source, out of 100, printed only for a company scored on all five factors.
- `presence`: Presence, out of 100.
- `named_in_answers`: How many of the edition's AI answers named the company.

These are results only. The data describes what AI engines said in the edition's answers; it does not explain why, and a low reading passes no judgement on a company.
